# glacial_scars

**PNG:** binary presence map — cyan = `glacial=true` (any LGM-related scar), grey = none, dark = ocean.

**CSV:** multi-tag `glacial_scar` field (semicolon-joined): none | u_valley | hanging_valley | overdeep_basin | moraine | outwash | scoured_craton | rebound_coast.

`glacial=true` iff `glacial_scar` is not `none`/empty (A17).
